• The Surface Reading. The deceptive, everyday meaning of the sentence designed to distract you. Ignore the story it tells and treat every word as a mechanical instruction. • The Literal (Definition). A direct synonym or meaning of the answer. It is almost always located at the very beginning or very end of the clue. • The Cryptic (Wordplay). The remaining part of the clue that gives you step-by-step instructions to build the answer letter-by-letter or word-by-word
